How it works

All of this is from my own closet and home.

  1. Claim it. Email hello@materialsmethods.ca or message me on Instagram with the item you want. First person to ask gets it, and I'll confirm.
  2. Pay. E-transfer to hello@materialsmethods.ca, or cash at pickup.
  3. Pick it up. I'll send my address and some times that work. It'll be on my porch in Waterloo.

The polished photos are the brand's original product shots. Every listing also has my own photos of the actual item, and I note any flaws.

Not sure about fit? I can measure things or take more photos. You're also welcome to try it on at pickup - no pressure if it doesn't work.
